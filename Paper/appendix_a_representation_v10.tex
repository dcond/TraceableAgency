\section{Proof of the representation theorem}
\label{app:main-proof}

This appendix gives one forward proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.  Its
dependency spine is simple.  We first derive the affine and behavioural tools
used throughout.  We then restrict attention to constant payoffs, align the
comparison scales across priors and support faces, and identify the resulting
trace value as mutual information.  Returning to the full payoff--record
domain, we put material utility and trace on one scale, prove the exact
reached-branch identity, and assemble an arbitrary channel by a finite
telescope and sequentialisation.

The constant-payoff phase deliberately precedes material calibration.  In
particular, Proposition~\ref{pt:prop:main} retains its weaker assumptions and
does not use material relevance.  Given marked-law sufficiency,
cardinalisation occurs only once: Axioms~\textup{(A1)}, \textup{(A2)}, and the
public-mixture case of~\textup{(A8)} yield an affine representative on each
full-support fibre.  Axioms~\textup{(A5)--(A7)} supply the transport and
cross-environment comparisons; the product and branch gauges, using the
finite-branch form of~\textup{(A8)}, align those fibrewise scales and produce
the exact chain rule.  Faddeev's entropy theorem then completes the
constant-payoff identification.
